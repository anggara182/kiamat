# Chapter 8 & 9 (final): tahap 4 "Pemimpin yang dinanti & perang besar"
Keputusan (16 Nov 2026, adrian: "lanjut"): taruhan naik dari sungai (Ch6–7) ke bumi yang menelan pasukan (Ch8) lalu perang dunia (Ch9). Ch10 "The Fall" (pertempuran besar, penaklukan, lalu teriakan bahwa Dajjal telah keluar) menutup tahap 4. Tokoh baru semua; tanpa shot HP; sang pemimpin tanpa wajah; Ka'bah tidak digambar (hanya cahaya); tidak ada simbol agama atau bendera di kedua pihak.

---

## CHAPTER 8: THE REFUGE (62.4 dtk)
**Kutipan di layar (shot 10):** "A seeker of refuge would seek refuge in the Sacred House and an army would be sent to him, and when it would enter a plain ground, it would be made to sink." (SAHIH MUSLIM 2882)
> An army marched into the desert to kill one man.
> The desert swallowed it.
> Weeks earlier, he was just a man in a quiet city, giving away gold without counting.
> People began to whisper that he was the one the Prophet had promised.
> A man from the Prophet's own family, who would fill the earth with justice... as it had been filled with oppression.
> He never claimed it. He ran.
> He fled to the one place no army should ever touch: the Sacred House in Mecca.
> But the men who feared him sent an army after him.
> Soldiers, trucks, armour. A column longer than the horizon.
> Fourteen centuries ago, the Prophet said a man would seek refuge in the House, an army would be sent against him, and when it reached open ground, the earth would swallow it.
> Then the sand began to tremble.
> And the ground opened.
> By morning, there was only flat sand... and tire tracks that led nowhere.
> In Mecca, a hundred thousand hands reached toward the man who had run.
> And far to the north, an empire signed a peace treaty it never meant to keep.

**Klasifikasi:**
- KANONIK: seorang yang berlindung di Baitullah, pasukan dikirim kepadanya, dan pasukan itu ditenggelamkan bumi saat sampai di tanah lapang (Muslim 2882; kutipan di layar). Seorang lelaki dari keluarga Nabi yang memenuhi bumi dengan keadilan sebagaimana sebelumnya dipenuhi kezaliman (Abu Dawud 4282, hasan sahih menurut al-Albani; diparafrasekan di VO).
- INTERPRETASI: menyamakan "orang yang berlindung" di Muslim 2882 dengan Mahdi (dilakukan banyak ulama, tetapi hadis ini tidak menyebut namanya); juga menyamakan pemberi harta tanpa hitungan di Ch7 (Muslim 2913) dengan orang yang sama. VO hanya menyebut "the one the Prophet had promised" dan tidak memakai kata Mahdi.
- FIKSI: kota, pasar, barikade, ruang gelap, ukuran pasukan, truk, jejak ban, seratus ribu tangan, dan perjanjian di utara.

## CHAPTER 9: EIGHTY BANNERS (69.1 dtk)
**Kutipan di layar (shot 10):** "Then there will be a truce between you and Bani Al-Asfar who will betray you and attack you under eighty flags. Under each flag will be twelve thousand soldiers." (SAHIH AL-BUKHARI 3176)
> Eighty banners. Under each one, twelve thousand soldiers.
> Nearly a million men, marching under one broken promise.
> It began with a handshake.
> A truce between the believers and the Romans, exactly as the Prophet had foretold.
> For years, it held. Ports opened. Markets filled. Children grew up without ever hearing a siren.
> Together, they even marched against a common enemy... and won.
> But on the day of victory, a quarrel broke out over whose victory it was.
> Within a week, the treaty was ash.
> The same ports that had sold them fruit now filled with their warships.
> The Prophet had said there would be a truce with the Romans. They would betray it, and come under eighty banners, under each banner twelve thousand.
> In every city, men answered the call. Farmers. Teachers. Boys too young to shave.
> Schools emptied. Factories fell silent. On every street, a mother stood at a window.
> They drove north through the night, toward a valley none of them had ever seen.
> And at dawn, the two armies faced each other, at a place the Prophet had named fourteen centuries before.

**Klasifikasi:**
- KANONIK: gencatan senjata dengan Bani al-Ashfar (Romawi) yang lalu dikhianati, dan mereka datang di bawah delapan puluh panji dengan dua belas ribu prajurit di bawah tiap panji (Bukhari 3176; kutipan di layar memakai terjemahan Khan, dan angka 12.000 juga ada di Ibnu Majah 4095, sahih). Damai bersama Romawi, lalu memerangi musuh bersama dan menang (Abu Dawud 4292, sahih).
- INTERPRETASI: "pertengkaran soal kemenangan siapa" meringkas Abu Dawud 4292; detail salib yang diangkat lalu dipatahkan sengaja tidak ditampilkan supaya tidak memancing permusuhan antaragama. Tempat yang "dinamai Nabi" (Muslim 2897: al-A'maq atau Dabiq) sengaja tidak disebut. "Romawi" tidak dipetakan ke negara modern mana pun. Urutan peristiwa sesudah Ch8 adalah pilihan dramatik.
- FIKSI: jabat tangan, alun-alun, pelabuhan, pasar, pertengkaran di kemah, perjanjian yang dibakar, kapal perang, relawan muda dan ibunya, ruang kelas kosong, dan konvoi malam.
