# Chapter 4 & 5 (draf 1): penutup tahap 2 "Fitnah & pendusta"

Taruhan naik: Ch3 satu kota → Ch4 satu negeri (amanah ke yang bukan ahlinya) → Ch5 ilmu dicabut dan berhala kembali → kail ke tahap 3 (harta melimpah). Tokoh baru tiap chapter, tanpa shot HP, sang "pria tanpa masa lalu" tetap tanpa wajah dan hanya terlihat dari jauh.

---

## CHAPTER 4: THE LOUDEST VOICE (skala: negeri)
**Kutipan di layar:** "When honesty is lost, then wait for the Hour." (Sahih al-Bukhari 59; dikonfirmasi lewat pencarian)

> In thirty days, his followers took eleven cities.
> They didn't need weapons. They only needed crowds.
> In every city, the same thing happened. The people who knew how things worked were pushed aside… and the loudest voices took their place.
> A man with no training ran the hospital. A man who could barely read rewrote the schools. A new bridge was built by people who had never built anything.
> On the day it opened, the crowds cheered.
> On the third night, it fell. No one was on it. Not yet.
> The engineers who warned them had already been silenced.
> A man once asked the Prophet when the Hour would come. He said: when trust is lost. And when authority is handed to those unfit for it.
> Across the country, the experts packed their bags. The loud ones stayed.
> And in the capital, the man from nowhere gave his next order: close the libraries.

**Visual kunci:** peta-tanpa-nama dengan titik-titik kota yang menyala satu per satu (gaya ilustrasi, tanpa negara nyata); dokter yang diusir dari rumah sakit dan diganti pria berteriak; guru yang papan tulisnya dihapus; jembatan baru dengan pita dan sorak; jembatan runtuh di malam kosong (tanpa korban); insinyur dengan gulungan cetak biru dibungkam (mulut ditutup tangan, bukan kekerasan); koper-koper di terminal; gerbang perpustakaan dirantai.
**Sumber:** Bukhari 59 (KANONIK). **Fiksi:** semua kota, tokoh, jembatan, perintah.

## CHAPTER 5: OLD GODS (skala: negeri, ke dalam rumah)
**Kutipan di layar:** "Religious knowledge will be taken away (by the death of Religious learned men)." (Sahih al-Bukhari 80; dikonfirmasi lewat pencarian)

> The libraries closed on a Monday. By Friday, no one remembered why they had been open.
> The old teachers grew quiet. One by one, they died. And no one took their place.
> Children learned chants instead of letters. Parents stopped asking questions they couldn't answer.
> And on the hill outside the city, something old was uncovered. A stone figure, buried for centuries.
> At first, people only came to look. Then they brought flowers. Then they began to walk around it… slowly… in circles.
> No one told them to. It just felt right.
> The Prophet said that before the Hour, knowledge would be taken away, ignorance would spread, and people would return to the idols their ancestors had abandoned.
> That spring, the rain came early. The harvest was the biggest anyone had ever seen.
> And with it came more money than anyone knew what to do with.

**Visual kunci:** rantai di gerbang perpustakaan; buku berdebu, kursi kosong di kelas; pemakaman guru tua yang sepi; anak-anak bertepuk mengikuti yel tanpa buku; arkeolog/warga menggali patung batu di bukit (patung generik, bukan dewa agama nyata mana pun); bunga di kaki patung; orang mengelilinginya pelan saat senja; hujan awal musim, sawah emas, karung uang menumpuk (kail ke tahap 3).
**Sumber:** Bukhari 80, 81 (ilmu dicabut, kebodohan) KANONIK; kembalinya penyembahan berhala: Bukhari 7116 / Muslim 2906 (Dzul Khalashah, dikonfirmasi) KANONIK, dipindahkan ke latar fiksi sebagai INTERPRETASI (hadis menyebut kabilah Daus di Jazirah Arab; seri tidak mengklaim patung ini adalah Dzul Khalashah). Hujan dan panen raya: FIKSI, jembatan ke Bukhari 7121 (harta melimpah) di Ch6.

## Catatan
- Baris "people would return to the idols their ancestors had abandoned" adalah parafrase ringkas dari Bukhari 7116 / Muslim 2906; tidak dikutip kata per kata.
- Kutipan di layar hanya yang sudah dikonfirmasi; sebelum tayang tetap dicocokkan dengan teks penuh sunnah.com.
