# EPISODE 01 (draft 5: English VO, fast-paced narrative)

Narrator outside the story, descriptive, fast-paced storytelling: short sentences, tight rhythm, pauses only on the key beats (marked `…`). No "I", no contrast pattern. Hook and title still open for discussion.

**Phase / block:** 01 / Block I · **Time:** 2026 · **Target length:** 60–80 s (this version ±175 words, about 62–68 s at a brisk storytelling pace)

## Delivery notes
- Pace: brisk and confident, like a trailer narrator who is telling a story, not reading the news. Clipped fragments ("Moving. Never stopping." / "Read. Swipe. Forgotten.") land on cuts.
- Slow down only on three beats: the end of shot 7 ("…fourteen hundred years ago."), shot 8, and the last line of shot 10.
- Tone stays serious and grounded. No horror whisper, no preaching.

## Transition
EP02 opens with the false message spreading faster than the truth. Fictional, local, not supernatural. This is the doorway into Phase 02: lies, panic, and the collapse of trust.

## Sources
- Competing in tall buildings: Muslim 8; Bukhari 50; Bukhari 7121
- Trust neglected, authority given to the unqualified: Bukhari 59
- Earthquakes increase, killing (harj) increases, time passes quickly: Bukhari 7121
- Only Allah knows when the Hour comes: Qur'an 7:187, 31:34

## Classification
- **CANONICAL:** the five conditions named in shot 7 (and shown in shots 3–6) with the sources above; shot 8.
- **INTERPRETATION:** none.
- **FICTIONAL:** the city, the people, all news items (no real events), and the false message in shot 10.

## Notes
- Shot 7 is a short paraphrase, not a hadith quotation. Attributing it to the Prophet is accurate because every item comes from the authentic hadith listed above. Any direct quotation later must use an established English translation word for word.
- Shot 4 deliberately names no real place or event.
- If the final VO runs past 80 s, cut "From the top, the people below are just dots…" first.
