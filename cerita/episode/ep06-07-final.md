# Chapter 6 & 7 (final): tahap 3 "Bumi & harta" (Efrat)
Keputusan (16 Nov 2026, setelah adrian minta taruhan dinaikkan dan tanda yang dikenal orang): Ch6 "Too Much" dan Ch7 "The Green Desert" di roadmap lama dilebur jadi latar; tahap 3 langsung ke tanda paling terkenal, Efrat menyingkap gunung emas. Hook keduanya kalimat paling keras di cerita ("The Euphrates…", "Ninety-nine out of every hundred…"). Tokoh baru semua; tanpa shot HP.

---

## CHAPTER 6: THE RIVER RUNS DRY (68.1 dtk)
**Kutipan di layar (shot 11):** "Soon the river Euphrates will disclose the treasure (the mountain) of gold, so whoever will be present at that time should not take anything of it." (SAHIH AL-BUKHARI 7119)
> The Euphrates had flowed for ten thousand years.
> Then, in one summer, it stopped.
> First, the boats sat on the mud.
> Then the fish came up gasping, by the thousands.
> The wells went dry. Whole villages loaded their lives onto trucks.
> A can of water cost more than a day's work.
> And the great river shrank to a thin brown thread.
> In the cities, water came by the cupful. In the fields, nothing grew.
> And the old men began to remember.
> Fourteen centuries ago, the Prophet said this river would one day uncover a mountain of gold.
> And he left one warning: whoever is there should take nothing from it.
> Most people laughed.
> Then a girl chasing her goat across the riverbed saw something shine.
> She dug with her hands. It didn't end.
> Her father dug. Then the whole village dug.
> It wasn't a coin. It wasn't a nugget. It went down further than anyone could see.
> By sunset, the poorest village on the river was sitting on more gold than every king in history.
> By nightfall, the news had crossed every border.
> By morning, a million people were walking toward the river.

**Klasifikasi:**
- KANONIK: Efrat menyingkap harta/gunung emas, dan larangan mengambil darinya (Bukhari 7119; Muslim 2894c/d). Kutipan di layar: terjemahan Khan untuk Bukhari 7119.
- INTERPRETASI: sungai mengering sebagai jalan tersingkapnya emas (para pensyarah menyebut kemungkinan surutnya air; teks hadis tidak merinci caranya).
- FIKSI: semua tokoh, desa, kekeringan musim panas, harga air, gadis dan kambing, sejuta orang berjalan. Tanpa batas negara atau peristiwa nyata; kekeringan Efrat masa kini TIDAK diklaim sebagai tanda.

## CHAPTER 7: NINETY-NINE (60.3 dtk)
**Kutipan di layar (shot 11):** "The Last Hour would not come before the Euphrates uncovers a mountain of gold, for which people would fight." (SAHIH MUSLIM 2894)
> Ninety-nine out of every hundred will die for it.
> And every one of them believes he'll be the one who survives.
> They came from every direction. Trucks, carts, whole families carrying shovels.
> Two friends from the same street dug side by side. On the first day, they shared their water.
> On the second day, they marked their ground with rope.
> On the third day, only one of them came back.
> Fathers stopped trusting their sons. Brothers stopped speaking.
> Then the soldiers came to guard the gold. And the soldiers fought each other.
> Every night, the camp grew a little quieter.
> Sandals left at tent doors. Shovels no one came back for.
> Fourteen centuries ago, the Prophet said people would fight over it. And ninety-nine of every hundred would be killed.
> Each one hoping... to be the one who lived.
> When the dust settled, the gold was still there. It didn't care who owned it.
> And far from the river, in a city no one was watching, a man was giving wealth away... without even counting it.
> And people had started to whisper a name.

**Klasifikasi:**
- KANONIK: orang-orang berperang memperebutkannya, 99 dari 100 terbunuh, tiap orang berharap dialah yang selamat (Muslim 2894a; Bukhari 7119). Kutipan di layar: kalimat pertama Muslim 2894a (terjemahan Siddiqi), dikonfirmasi lewat pencarian.
- INTERPRETASI: kail ke tahap 4. "Seorang pria membagikan harta tanpa menghitungnya" mengacu pada khalifah akhir zaman yang membagi harta tanpa hitungan (Muslim 2913/2914); para ulama mengaitkannya dengan Mahdi. Seri belum menyebut nama dan tidak mengklaim urutan waktu pasti terhadap peristiwa Efrat (pilihan dramatik).
- FIKSI: dua sahabat, ayah-anak, tentara tanpa bendera, kemah, kota jauh. Kekerasan hanya tersirat (tanpa jasad, darah, atau luka).
